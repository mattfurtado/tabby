# Tabby privacy policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Tabby runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, send or sell any data.

## What Tabby reads

- **Tab titles, URLs and favicons** in your current window, so it can suggest groups and flag tabs that are safe to close.
- **The open/merged/closed label on GitHub pull request pages** you have open, so it can flag merged or closed PRs.

## What Tabby stores

Tabby saves its settings, suggestions and the last known state of GitHub pull requests in `chrome.storage.local` on your computer. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## AI features

If you turn on AI suggestions, Tabby uses Chrome's built-in on-device language model. Tab titles and URLs are processed on your computer and are not sent to any server.

## Network and third parties

Tabby makes no network requests of its own, uses no analytics, and shares nothing with third parties.
